Features

Munnar

Munnar is a beautiful hill station famous for its tea plantations, misty mountains and cool climate.

Alleppey

Alleppey is known for its peaceful backwaters, traditional houseboats and beautiful waterways.

Wayanad

Wayanad is a peaceful destination filled with forests, waterfalls, wildlife and scenic landscapes.

Kovalam

Kovalam is a famous coastal destination known for its golden beaches, palm trees and sunsets.
